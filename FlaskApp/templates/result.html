Understanding Your Results

The combined stage reflects overall risk:

  • Stage 2: Both conditions present – highest concern.

  • Stage 1 (Heart Risk): Only heart risk detected.
  • Normal: Neither detected.
Important: This analysis is not a definitive diagnosis. Always consult with a healthcare professional.
